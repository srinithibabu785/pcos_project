5. DATA LEAKAGE RISKS & RESOLUTION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------
RISK: If random train/test splitting was applied to the raw archive, exact duplicates
and rotated/resized twins of the same physical scan would enter both Train and Test.
This would produce an artificially inflated, fraudulent accuracy (e.g. 99-100%)
while completely failing on unseen clinical data.
CROSS-LABEL CONFLICT: 18 perceptual groups have identical visual content but contradictory
labels (e.g., infected/image10441.jpg vs noninfected/Image_333.jpg, mean pixel diff 0.98/255).
RESOLUTION:
1. Quarantine and remove the 18 contradictory cross-class ground-truth conflict pairs.
2. De-duplicate all redundant binary copies.
3. Group all augmented variants into strict Image Families (Group-aware splitting).
4. Enforce ZERO family overlap across Train, Validation, and Test sets.
5. Keep Test set 100% isolated, un-augmented, and unseen until final evaluation.

6. LABEL SEMANTICS VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------
AUDIT VERDICT:
Label semantics could not be independently verified from the supplied dataset.
No clinical protocol, institutional ethics documentation, or physician codebook
was included in datasett.zip.
In open-source ML conventions, 'infected' is used colloquially for PCOS-positive
(polycystic ovarian morphology) and 'noninfected' for PCOS-negative (normal morphology).
For academic integrity, this pipeline treats these as research proxy labels and frames
the system as an Explainable AI Clinical Decision Support prototype, not a definitive diagnosis.
